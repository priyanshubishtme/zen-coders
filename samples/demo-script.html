Weekly chaos

Grey text on white that fails contrast.

Pastries sell out by nine.

Free delivery never quite reaches the contrast line.

Second sitting is worse.

The chalkboard is unreadable.

Prices fade into the wall.

Even the allergen note fails.

The gluten-free tray is a rumour.

Closing time is printed too light to read.

Staff names wash out on the rota.

The tip jar sign is a rumour too.

Today's soup is written in fog.

The wifi password is a pale whisper.

Monday's hours are a grey smudge.

The allergy sheet is fog on paper.
